Three things the numbers do not say out loud.
Milan is not Turin. The €40–€50 per-video figure is a Turin benchmark. Quoting it to a Milan agency leaves money on the table.
"From €5,000" means outsourcing. Agencies with high minimums rarely shoot small jobs in-house. That overflow is where freelance work comes from.

Is €1,200–€1,500 defensible in Turin?

Short answer: yes.

At an average finished length of two minutes, thirty videos is roughly sixty minutes of delivered content per month. A €1,200–€1,500 retainer holds up in the Turin market when filming, editing, revisions, file management and delivery are all included. Push the number up when the brief adds shooting days, higher production quality, complex editing, or social media management on top of the footage.
